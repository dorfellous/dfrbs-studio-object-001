# DFRBS Studio — positioning and service strategy

Strategy proposal, 5 October 2026. The collaborator confirmed **DFRBS Studio**, a joint studio for **Ran Bensimon and Dor Fellous**, serving **brands, artists and cultural organisations**. This document aligns with `src/studioServices.js`; it does not change website behaviour.

## Positioning

Recommended position: **a creative studio combining artistic direction and creative technology to make films, digital fashion, sculptural objects and interactive experiences**. Lead with the client’s outcome and the work’s visual identity; explain the production method inside the relevant offer or case study.

Use **Websites & creative tools** as the client-facing label. “Vibe coding” can explain a method when useful, but does not describe the user task, production scope or handover.

## Likely commission scenarios — proposals

- **Brand launch:** visual direction + selected 3D/digital-fashion assets + film; add a campaign website for a defined audience task. Begin with a treatment and a small visual study.
- **Artist release/performance:** film or coordinated stage-screen imagery. Begin with one song/sequence and confirm playback formats.
- **Cultural programme:** moving-image artwork, sculptural contribution or screen-based prototype. Begin with an artistic research pilot and representative setting test.
- **Recurring production task:** documented image/video workflow + team walkthrough. Begin with sample material, quality criteria and a decision on whether to continue.

## Engagement models — proposals

1. **Defined project:** a scoped outcome with milestones, approval rounds, formats and acceptance checks. Ends with agreed delivery and handover.
2. **Research pilot:** a bounded question tested through a sample/prototype. Ends with evidence, limitations and a recommendation to continue, revise or stop.
3. **Ongoing partnership:** recurring priorities and agreed delivery batches for evolving imagery, tools or workflows. Agree review cadence, support boundaries and renewal; do not imply unlimited output or permanent availability.

Fees, dates, founder allocation and capacity are agreed per brief; no studio-wide commercial assumptions are established by the evidence.

## Scope and handover

Every proposal should define outcome/audience; supplied inputs and decision-maker; output formats/versions; production boundaries and collaborator needs; final exports versus editable/source/reusable files; dependencies/setup notes; and post-delivery hosting, publishing, account ownership, maintenance or on-site operation.
